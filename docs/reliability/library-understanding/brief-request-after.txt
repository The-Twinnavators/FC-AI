Product: Studio Bookings
Request: A booking app for a small yoga studio

PRD (people, goals, journeys, screens):
# Studio Bookings PRD

## Users
- Visitors: people new to the studio deciding whether to try a class.
- Members: people with a pass who book and cancel classes each week.
- Staff: front-desk staff and teachers who see who is coming to each class.

## Goals
Members see the week's classes, book a spot and cancel up to two hours before class. Staff see who is coming to each
class and check people in. Visitors understand the studio, its passes and prices, and book a first class.

## Passes
Drop-in $22, 10-class pass $190, unlimited monthly $129. The first class is free.

## Screens
| Screen | Purpose |
| Home | Visitors learn what the studio offers, see this week's classes and the passes, and book a first class |
| Classes | Members see the week's schedule, filter by type and teacher, and book a spot (shows spots left; full classes offer the waitlist) |
| My bookings | Members see upcoming bookings and cancel up to two hours before class |
| Class roster | Staff see who is booked for a class and check them in |

## Requirements
- R1 Weekly schedule with class type, teacher, time and spots left.
- R2 Book a spot; a full class offers the waitlist.
- R3 Cancel up to two hours before class; later cancellation is refused with the reason.
- R4 Staff roster per class with check-in.
- R5 Passes and prices on the home page; first class free.

## Home

Parts for this screen (what each does, in the catalog's words):
- p.booking-calendar (pattern, Booking calendar: a day, a time, booked): days marked by how much is left, the chosen day's times as a radio group with places left, a named time zone, and a confirmation that says what was booked and lets the person chang. Belongs on app screens and marketing pages. Gives a page: booking, date entry, time entry. Needs: the times free on each day
- b.booking-flow (block, Appointment booking flow): A whole appointment booking (b.booking-flow) for a salon, studio or clinic: pick a service, a staff member or "Anyone available", a day and a free time, then your details;. Belongs on marketing pages and app screens. Gives a page: booking, steps, date entry, time entry, form, review, edit, primary action, error state. Needs: services with length, price in cents and who does them; staff, opening hours and closed days; which start times are taken. Avoid when: only a day and a time to pick (use p.booking-calendar); classes with a fixed timetable many people join (use an event listing)
- p.upcoming-schedule (pattern, Upcoming schedule): a dashboard card of upcoming appointments or events, soonest first, each with day and time, title, detail and a status badge, a box to mark it done and a Hide done toggle.. Belongs on app screens and marketing pages. Gives a page: results. Needs: dated appointments or events with a status. Avoid when: the person needs to pick a day or time: use the booking calendar
- b.newsletter-inline (block, Newsletter sign-up): An inline email sign-up band that checks the address, confirms and keeps it locally.. Belongs on marketing pages
- c.modal (component, Modals: confirm, destructive, form and the one you cannot dismiss): The one dialog over the page (c.modal), with focus trapping, scroll lock, Escape and focus return built in;. Belongs on app screens and marketing pages. Also as: c.modal.confirm, c.modal.danger, c.modal.form, c.modal.blocking
