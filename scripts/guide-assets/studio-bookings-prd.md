# Studio Bookings

## 1. Problem
Members of a small yoga studio can't see which classes have spots left, and staff don't know who is coming.

## 2. Users
- Members who book classes
- Staff who run classes and mark attendance

## 3. Requirements
### R1 — Week schedule
Members see this week's classes by day, with spots left.
### R2 — Book a spot
Booking a class with spots left adds it to My bookings.
### R3 — Cancel
Members can cancel up to two hours before class; the spot frees up.
### R4 — Class list for staff
Staff see who is booked into each class.
### R5 — Attendance
Staff mark each member present or absent.
